# Workout Cardio Policy V1

The backend decides weekly cardio slots before exercise selection.

## Weekly maximum

- 2-4 training days: 1 cardio session
- 5-6 training days: 2 cardio sessions

Weight-loss and fitness plans use the maximum when the session budget supports at least five total exercises. Muscle, strength, and body-shape plans may use fewer sessions so cardio does not displace required resistance work.

Cardio is always the final workout slot. Weight-loss and fitness plans prefer day 1, plus day 4 when two sessions are used, to separate exposure and avoid lower or leg days when the split offers an alternative. Muscle, strength, and body-shape plans retain a single optional final-day session so the existing resistance skeleton remains stable.

The policy limits frequency only. Existing duration, equipment, candidate, injury, and low-impact validation remains active.

Run validation with:

```bash
npm run validate:workout-cardio
```
